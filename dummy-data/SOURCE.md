# Dummy office data provenance

The 140 records in this folder are **synthetic demo records**. They are intended to make the SIH prototype realistic without pretending to be a live government master.

The field structure is inspired by fields commonly present in postal-office directories (for example circle/region/division, office name, PIN, office type, delivery, district and state). This project does not claim that any individual synthetic row is a current official record.

Reference sources:
- India Post Pincode List: https://www.indiapost.gov.in/rti/pincodelist
- Government of India OGD All India Pincode Directory: https://www.data.gov.in/catalog/all-india-pincode-directory-through-webservice

Before production, replace this dataset with a current, verified Department of Posts master or approved API/feed and store its source version/date.
